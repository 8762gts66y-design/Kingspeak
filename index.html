Kings Peak — 13,528 ft
Utah's highest point · Uinta Mountains · 2-day backpacking trip
28-29 mi
5,000 ft

About
Utah's highest summit at 13,528 ft in the Uinta Mountains — northeastern Utah, about 3 hours from Pleasant Grove. Standard route via Henry's Fork trailhead. The first 10+ miles are a long gradual valley hike through Henry's Fork Basin before the real climb begins. Most people camp at Dollar Lake (~10 miles in) and summit on day two. The altitude is what gets people — not the steepness. If you've done Timp with a pack consistently, you're ready.
